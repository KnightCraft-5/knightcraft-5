================================================================
  KNIGHTCRAFT 5 - KURULUM / INSTALLATION
  Minecraft 1.20.1  |  Forge 47.4.10
================================================================

--- TURKCE ----------------------------------------------------

1) Minecraft 1.20.1 icin FORGE 47.4.10 kur.
   https://files.minecraftforge.net  ->  1.20.1  ->  47.4.10

   Onemli: Forge'u kurduktan sonra oyunu Forge profiliyle BIR KEZ
   ac ve ana menude kapat. Boylece .minecraft klasoru hazirlanir.

2) Bu zip'in icindekileri .minecraft klasorunun UZERINE cikar.
   Sorulursa "birlestir / uzerine yaz" de.

   Windows : %appdata%\.minecraft
   Linux   : ~/.minecraft
   macOS   : ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft

3) Oyuna en az 6 GB RAM ver. Launcher > Profil > Ayarlar >
   JVM argumanlari:  -Xmx6G

4) Oyunu ac. Ilk acilis 3-5 dakika surebilir, normaldir.

5) DILI TURKCE YAP:
   Options > Language > Turkce (Turkiye)
   Bu paketin gorev kitabi ve 17 modun cevirisi Turkce dilinde
   hazirlandi. Dili degistirmezsen hepsi Ingilizce gorunur.

6) Sunucuya baglan ve /register <sifre> <sifre> yaz.
   Her giriste /login <sifre> gerekir.

--- ENGLISH ---------------------------------------------------

1) Install FORGE 47.4.10 for Minecraft 1.20.1, then launch the
   Forge profile ONCE and quit at the main menu so that the
   .minecraft folder is populated.

2) Extract the contents of this zip ON TOP of .minecraft,
   choosing "merge / overwrite" if prompted.

3) Allocate at least 6 GB of RAM  ( -Xmx6G ).

4) Launch. First start takes 3-5 minutes.

5) SET THE LANGUAGE TO TURKISH: Options > Language > Turkce.
   The quest book and 17 mods' translations are Turkish-only;
   leaving the game in English hides all of it.

6) On the server, run /register <password> <password> once,
   then /login <password> on every join.

--- NE ICERIR / WHAT IS INCLUDED ------------------------------

  mods/           tum modlar / all mods
  config/         ayarlar + gorev kitabi (config/ftbquests)
  defaultconfigs/ yeni dunyalara kopyalanan sunucu ayarlari
  kubejs/         tarifler, PMMO kapilari, Turkce ceviriler
  packmenu/       menu gorseli

  Kaydedilmis dunyalar, ayarlar (options.txt), sunucu listesi ve
  kisisel dosyalar BILEREK dahil edilmedi - kendi ayarlarinin
  uzerine yazmasin diye.

  Saves, options.txt, the server list and personal caches are
  deliberately NOT included so the pack cannot clobber your own
  settings.
